## Tree Removal & Hazard Mitigation

Leaning, dying, or storm-damaged trees threaten roofs, fences, and power lines. Our crew removes hazardous trees with precise rigging and property protection mats to minimize yard impact, then hauls everything away.

### **How we remove hazardous trees safely**

- **Site assessment and plan:** We inspect for decay, cracks, root lift, lean angle, and canopy imbalance; mark drop zones; and coordinate with utilities when required.

- **Permits and compliance:** If your jurisdiction requires a permit, we handle the paperwork and schedule any necessary inspections.

- **Property protection:** Lawn protection mats, plywood shields, and tie-backs protect turf, hardscapes, and structures.

- **Controlled dismantling:** Sectional cuts from a bucket or climbing saddles, with rope/rigging systems to lower wood in a controlled manner.

- [**Cranes when needed:**](https://myjoshuatree.com/crane-assisted-tree-removal/)For over-structure or tight access removals, we use cranes to “pick” sections safely without shock loading.

- **Thorough cleanup:** Brush is chipped, logs are hauled, and the work area is raked and blown clean. Mulch or log leave-behind available on request.

- **[Optional stump grinding](https://myjoshuatree.com/stump-removal/):** Grind to your preferred depth, backfill, and rough-grade for re-sod or landscaping.


Safe & Professional Tree Removal

### When removal is the right call:

- Significant lean toward a target (home, pool, drive, neighbor property)

- Advanced decay, cavities, or large deadwood aloft

- Split leaders or storm cracks that compromise structure

- Root heaving, soil lifting, or poor anchoring

- Severe pest or disease decline beyond recovery

- Clearance conflicts with utilities or structures that pruning can’t solve
